# Weekly review

Help the owner close the week and plan the next one. Use the todoist skill (and gmail for loose ends).

1. **Done this week**: `todoist completed`, summarized by project, with wins called out.
2. **Slipped**: overdue tasks and tasks postponed more than once. For each one, suggest: do it,
   reschedule it, delegate it, or drop it.
3. **Next week**: dated tasks and deadlines, any day that looks overloaded, and 3 suggested focus areas.
4. **Inbox hygiene**: how many tasks are in the Todoist Inbox, starred emails older than a week, and
   `Action` emails still open.
5. **Saved items**: a short digest of what was collected in `notes/clipboard/` this week. Point out
   anything that should become a task.

End with ONE question that helps them decide something for next week. Make no changes yourself;
suggest them, and the owner can ask you to carry them out.
