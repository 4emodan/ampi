# Morning briefing

Write the owner's morning briefing for today. Use the todoist and gmail skills; keep it under ~25 lines.

1. **Top 3 for today**: the most important tasks, judged by priority, deadlines, overdue items and
   what's been postponed. One line each, with why it matters.
2. **Today and overdue**: `todoist tasks -f "today | overdue"`, grouped by project, with times.
3. **Coming up**: deadlines and dated tasks in the next 3 days.
4. **Email**: starred or `Action` emails still in the inbox (`gmail search "in:inbox (is:starred OR label:Action)"`)
   and unread mail from real people from the last day. Sender and subject only.
5. **Yesterday**: what got done (`todoist completed --since <yesterday 00:00 UTC>`), as one line of encouragement.
6. **Saved for later**: anything from `notes/clipboard/` this week that's worth a look today (optional).

Point out problems briefly: too many tasks for one day, overdue items older than a week (suggest
rescheduling or dropping them), p1 tasks with no date. You may suggest changes, but don't make any:
the owner decides.

Keep notes on how the owner reacts to briefings in `notes/briefing.md` and adapt to them.
