Address book

Places Ampi can use by name: maps route work home, wolt venues --at gym, filling a delivery address. Ampi never sees coordinates; addresses only with your approval.

    Pick a place, or add one.

    The address changed: the location may still point at the old one. Press “Find from address”, or clear the location.

    “home” is the place used when you don't name one, and its timezone sets your schedules.
